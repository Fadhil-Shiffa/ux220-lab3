Best Movie I've Seen Lately

Project Hail Mary

One of the best movies I have seen lately is Project Hail Mary. I liked it because it mixes science fiction, mystery, and survival in a way that keeps the story interesting from beginning to end.

What I liked most was the problem-solving in the movie. The main character has to understand what is happening around him and use science to solve problems that could affect the entire planet. I enjoy stories where the character has to think through difficult situations instead of just relying on action.

I also liked the emotional side of the story. Even though a lot of the movie takes place in space, it still focuses on friendship, trust, and working together. That balance between science, humour, and emotion is what made the movie memorable for me.
